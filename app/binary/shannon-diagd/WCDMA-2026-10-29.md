The byte counters are interval totals. HSDPA reports cover 200 ms, corroborated by 100 two-ms decoding attempts. The supported gate is ICD 900, stack 1, at least 244 bytes, carrier count 1, attempts 100. HSUPA gates on ICD 900, stack 0, at least 56 bytes and interval 1000 ms. Counts greater than the supported carrier count or other intervals remain unsupported.

For every completed one-second receive bucket, average the HSDPA report rates ByteCount*40/1e6 and HSUPA report rates ByteCount*8/1e6. Summing sparse/bursty arrivals as though they necessarily span a complete second disagrees with NSG. An absent report produces null, while a reported zero remains zero. No cumulative-counter rollover logic is applied to interval totals.

Modulation usage pools the first-cell block counts. HS-SCCH success is 100*valid/attempts; it is not DL transport-block BLER. CQI is the count-weighted mean of the 31 bins, pooling all three histogram categories, and is explicitly labelled weighted. It does not reproduce NSG's separate CQIAVG calculation.

CPICH serving values are supported by the resolved getters and valid ranges. NSG exports measurements labelled After RAKE, which have different precision/aggregation; do not claim the CPICH integer samples exactly reproduce those fields. Tx power, RSSI, SIR and UL/DL BLER were investigated but were not added from unconfirmed offsets.

## Scope and checks

Single-carrier HSPA capture validated. Dual-carrier/DC-HSPA, other ICD versions/stack mappings and other report periods are not enabled by guesswork. WCDMA reports lack a verified UARFCN/SIM identity here. With multiple SIMs, the app accepts the sole WCDMA subscription and checks its known PSC when available; two WCDMA subscriptions remain ambiguous. Live device packets have no ICD envelope and enter with icd=0, so the same validated layout signatures are accepted for both untagged live packets and ICD-900 replay records.

Direct startup now also sends DMConsole's SelectHspaItem/RefreshHspaItem(ItemOption.EnableAll): commands 0x4000/0x4200 with one-byte payload 0xff. These were recovered from static CIL methods 06000da7/06000da9 and the ItemOption enum (DisableAll=0, EnableAll=255). Existing LTE selection remains. This enables the HSPA category as a whole; it is not a per-message subscription mask. The relay 0.1.7 activation profile has not been updated in this app change: joining an existing HSPA stream works, but broker-owned HSPA selection remains separate follow-up work.

A bounded live receive check on the connected phone produced eight WCDMA snapshots with actual DL/UL rates (including 3.179350 Mbps DL and 0.411200 Mbps UL), 1,101 frames, 68 decoded and zero malformed/framing errors. The user confirmed the app graph works after installation. The temporary diagnostic executables were removed. The DIAG chart now remains visible while waiting for reports instead of disappearing.

Native WCDMA tests passed under ASan/UBSan for interval averaging, missing windows, malformed counts and unsupported layouts. Existing NR uplink checks also passed after the telemetry changes. The staging APK built and installed successfully. Both focused WcdmaSnapshotTest tests passed using testDebugUnitTest: WCDMA rates exclude stale LTE/NR rates, and missing reports remain null.
